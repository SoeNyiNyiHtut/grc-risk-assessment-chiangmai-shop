# GRC Risk Assessment – Small Electronics Business (Chiang Mai)

## Overview
This project presents a practical Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) risk assessment for a small electronics reseller in Chiang Mai, Thailand.

The objective is to identify key assets, assess risks, and recommend practical security controls suitable for a small business environment.

---

## Business Context
- Small electronics reseller (~8 employees)
- Sales via Facebook Marketplace and Shopee
- Uses Google Workspace (Gmail, Google Drive, Google Sheets)
- Customer data stored in shared spreadsheets
- No dedicated IT/security team
- Limited budget and informal processes

---

## Scope
- Customer data protection
- Online account security
- Endpoint device usage
- Basic network security

---

## Asset Identification
- Customer Data
- Facebook Page
- Shopee Account
- Email Accounts
- Company Laptops
- Cloud Storage (Google Drive)
- Admin Credentials
- WiFi Network

---

## Risk Assessment Methodology
- Likelihood (1–5)
- Impact (1–5)
- Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact

Risk Levels:
- 1–5 = Low
- 6–14 = Medium
- 15–25 = High

---

## Conclusion
Small businesses face real cybersecurity risks due to limited controls.  
Simple improvements such as MFA, strong passwords, and awareness can significantly reduce risk.
